Squish Index Build 1.0 · 8 screens · as implemented

A specimen catalogue for objects that are inherently silly and soft.

These are the screens the SwiftUI actually draws, rendered against the same locked tokens: putty canvas, ink type, hairlines instead of shadows, and no brand accent anywhere. Every colour you can see comes out of a specimen. Screens 1–4 were mocked before the build; 5–8 are new.

Plates hold drawn stand-ins rather than photographs — the shapes match the six silhouette classes the app assigns. Screen 4’s durometer is live: drag it, or focus it and use the arrow keys.

Palette
Five tokens, no accent. #F1F0EC putty, #14151A ink, #74777E soft, #DDDBD4 line, #FFFFFF chalk.
Type
Bricolage Grotesque names things, Inter explains them, DM Mono measures them. The split is load-bearing.
Geometry
15 pt cards, 19–24 pt plates, 26 pt sheet tops, 13 pt gutter, 22 pt margin.
The signature
Ten bars that compress with area conserved. Width 12 → 26 pt, height by the reciprocal.
