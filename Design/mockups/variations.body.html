Squish Index · direction study

Five ways to build the index.

The same app, the same data, five positions on what a catalogue of soft objects should look like. Each direction is shown twice: the collection, and one specimen. Nothing here is decorated — every rule, column and figure is carrying information.

Specimens are drawn as flat object studies rather than photographs, so the shapes stand in for what the camera will supply. No faces, no highlights, no gradients. Pick one and it becomes the build.
