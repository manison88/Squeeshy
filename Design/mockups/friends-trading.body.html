Friends and trading, on iPhone and iPad

Visual target for Design/FRIENDS-AND-TRADING.md. Friend each other, browse each other's shelves, request a trade, then mark the hand-over. Same palette, type and components as the catalogue — every colour here still comes from a squishy.

The sample collection

Twenty-two sample squishies across four shelves: yours, Zoe's, Maya's and Ren's. Every screen below uses them. The palette strip under each one is pulled from the toy itself, which is where all the colour in the app comes from.

Toys still change hands in person or by post. The checklist uses the same box states as the Measuring screen. When both boxes are ticked, each squishy is filed in its new owner's catalogue.

iPad

On iPad the library and friends share a sidebar, with a friend's shelf open beside it. The grid goes to five columns, and trade requests open as a centred form sheet instead of a full screen. Try it: pick a friend in the sidebar, tap Request, choose what to offer, and send.
