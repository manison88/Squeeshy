Trade table animations

Three moments from the trade table, played with the same timings as the Swift code. Press Replay to watch again. In the third one, tap your side of the table to open it. The squeeshies are illustrations; the app uses real cut-outs.

Both say yes

Mia's yes lands. Each side lifts, crosses in an arc through a glow of the table's colours, and settles on the other seat. Then the stamp slams down, confetti in the traded squeeshies' colours, and what you got bounces in.

Someone says no

Mia says no. The stamp slams onto the board, it shakes, and a sweep wipes it clean. Both sides empty and the votes reset, so you can put in something new.

More than one squeeshy

Five on your side: three show, plus a "+2" tile and a count. Tap the seat and it opens to show all of them; the minus takes one off the table.
