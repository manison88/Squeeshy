Trade table moments

Eight ways the trade table could play out. Each phone runs the real flow: you vote, Mia votes, the animation plays, then it lands on the success screen or refreshes the board with who said no. Phones loop on their own while they're on screen. Tap a button under one to play that ending, which stops its loop.

  1. 1 Both put a squeeshy in
  2. 2 You vote, then Mia votes
  3. 3 The animation
  4. 4 Success screen, or a cleared board that says who said no

The squeeshies are illustrations; the app uses the real cut-outs. Pick the ones you like and I'll build them into the trade table.
