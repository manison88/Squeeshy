Squeeshy is a free catalogue for squishy toy collections.

Photograph a squishy and the app lifts it off its background on the device, then files it by colour, size, shape, and a "squeeshiness" rating you set with a slider. Shelves build themselves from those traits — every warm-coloured one, every large one, everything added this week — so the collection sorts itself, with no folders or tags to keep up.

Tap any squishy to open it full screen and squeeze it.

No account, no purchases, no ads. Everything stays on your device.
