**2. Devices and operating systems tested**

Tested on iPhone 15, 16, 16 Pro, 17, and 17 Pro running iOS 26, and on iPad Pro 13-inch (latest model) running iPadOS 26. Squeeshy requires iOS 26.0 or later and is universal, with a dedicated split-view layout on iPad.

**3. Purpose and audience**

Squeeshy is a free personal catalogue for squishy toy collections, aimed at collectors — mostly children, teenagers, and their parents. It solves the problem that a collection has no record of what is in it and its photos are lost in the camera roll: the user photographs a squishy and the app isolates it from its background and files it automatically by colour, size, shape, and a "squeeshiness" rating.

**4. Setup and access**

No login credentials or sample files are needed — there are no accounts, no sign-in, and no server, so the app is fully functional the moment it opens. To see the core feature, tap the camera button at the top right and photograph any small object (a squishy toy is ideal, but any handheld object works), then name it and save; it is filed automatically and appears on the Collections screen and in any shelf whose traits it matches.

**5. External services**

None. The app makes no network requests, contains no networking code, and works entirely offline — no data providers, no authentication, no payment processing, and no third-party AI service. Background removal runs on the device using Apple's Vision framework, all data is stored locally with SwiftData, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDK.

**6. Regional differences**

None. Squeeshy is a single build that behaves identically in every region, is in English only, is free everywhere, and has no server or location data that could vary its behaviour.

**7. Regulated industry and third-party material**

Neither applies. Squeeshy is not in a regulated industry, and all code, artwork, and copy are original and owned by 312 IT Consulting — the only images in the app are photographs users take of their own possessions, which never leave the device.
